# Round 2 rebuttal: The Adversarial (Gemini 4 Argon)

## 1. Strongest point accepted from another member

I accept **The First-Principle's** proof that switching profiles mid-run breaks cross-family reviewer independence unless the controller enforces a cumulative provenance ledger across the worktree's full artifact lineage ([Matrix](https://github.com/bughunt8/model-mesh/blob/daf9ac42a9e86700a2f6c1c8b10353cd998a2a09/docs/research/orchestration/MATRIX.md)). In Situation A01, `ultimate` assigns `deepseek` (`deepseek-v4`) to author seats (`sisyphus-junior`, `atlas`, `librarian`, `explore`), whereas `hybrid` assigns `deepseek` (`deepseek-v4-reasoner`) to `reviewer_panel` ([Matrix](https://github.com/bughunt8/model-mesh/blob/daf9ac42a9e86700a2f6c1c8b10353cd998a2a09/docs/research/orchestration/MATRIX.md)). Switching from `ultimate` to `hybrid` between iterations puts a DeepSeek reviewer over DeepSeek-authored commits from an earlier iteration.

This changes my recommendation in two ways: profile and situation option are frozen at admission, and `scripts/superloop.py` plus `scripts/materialize.py` must record `author_families_seen` and `author_harnesses_seen` across iterations to prune any reviewer or aggregator rung overlapping any prior author in that worktree ([Orchestration rules](https://github.com/bughunt8/model-mesh/blob/daf9ac42a9e86700a2f6c1c8b10353cd998a2a09/docs/ORCHESTRATION.md)).

## 2. Three claims by other members I reject

### Claim 1: Point thresholds or ScaleDown Extract are safe in the decision and state path (The Practical, The Explainer, The First-Principle)

The Practical gates completion on `noul goal_complete` (`P >= 0.8`), while The Explainer and The First-Principle put ScaleDown's `Extract` inside `scripts/build-state.py` to pull error spans from 1M-token logs. Both fail primary-source verification.

First, an October 2026 audit found that point-estimate gates broke their accuracy promise in 147 of 190 exchangeable evaluations and held an English-calibrated 95 percent gate in only 8 of 51 non-English locales, whereas Learn-then-Test held in all 190 ([Confidence Is Not a Promise](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36311v1)). Moreover, Jev Choice `confidence` is \((p_{\max} - 1/n)/(1 - 1/n)\) ([TypeSafe confidence docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence.md)), whereas ScaleDown `/v1/scaledown` Choice `confidence` is \(p_{\max}\) directly and its questions fan out into separate `/classify` calls whose tokens sum for billing ([ScaleDown Decisions API](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/decisions), [ScaleDown Decisions overview](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/decisions-overview)).

Second, the [ScaleDown Extract specification](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/extract) exposes two bugs:
1. The schema defines `start` and `end` as character offsets, while Notes states they "refer to byte positions in the original `text` string" ([ScaleDown Extract docs](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/extract)), breaking `source[start:end] == span` on UTF-8 or Chinese/Cantonese logs.
2. Scalar properties in `Extract` return "at most 30 distinct values each" in order of appearance, or a single value when combined with any array or nested object ([ScaleDown Extract docs](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/extract)). Emitting 30 benign warnings before a fatal security failure causes `Extract` to drop error 31 before the decider sees the state.

### Claim 2: OMO's Goal hook enforces a 100-iteration cap on dev (The Researcher) or enforced it in v4.19.4 (The Explainer)

The Researcher reported OMO's `Goal` loop has "a default continuation cap of 100" citing `dev/docs/reference/features.md`, while The Explainer claimed `v4.19.4` enforced `goal.default_max_iterations` at 100 and lost it on `dev`. Both trusted prose without inspecting the hook code across tags.

Re-verifying [v4.19.4 `src/hooks/goal/index.ts`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/v4.19.4/packages/omo-opencode/src/hooks/goal/index.ts) and [dev `src/hooks/goal/index.ts`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/bd1310b65df8d772bba604e958e79956dc3e89a2/packages/omo-opencode/src/hooks/goal/index.ts) proves the two files are byte-for-byte identical (105 lines, 96 LOC). In both versions, `handleSessionIdle` checks only `goal.status !== "active"` and dispatches `buildContinuationPrompt(goal)` on every `session.idle` with zero iteration counter and zero call to `controller.accountUsage` ([v4.19.4 `goal/controller.ts`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/v4.19.4/packages/omo-opencode/src/hooks/goal/controller.ts)). Between [v4.19.4 `features.md`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/v4.19.4/docs/reference/features.md) and [dev `features.md`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/dev/docs/reference/features.md), `dev` simply admitted that "the Goal hook doesn't enforce an iteration cap."

### Claim 3: `madewithjev.com` is TypeSafe's vendor guide (The Practical), and `oh-my-openagent run` is a one-step wrapper without disabling hooks (The Explainer)

The Practical cited [madewithjev.com/jev-multi-agent](https://madewithjev.com/jev-multi-agent) as "the vendor's own guide," but Jon Kraayenbrink's footer states: "Made with Jev is independent and not affiliated with TypeSafe AI." Meanwhile, The Explainer invoked each worker step via `bunx oh-my-openagent run` without disabling OMO's continuation hooks; re-verifying [dev `docs/reference/cli.md`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/dev/docs/reference/cli.md) and [v4.19.4 `docs/reference/cli.md`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/v4.19.4/docs/reference/cli.md) shows `oh-my-openagent run` exits only when the session is idle, all todos are completed or cancelled, all descendant sessions are idle, and no continuation hooks (`boulder`, `ralph-loop`, or `todo-hook` markers) are active, trapping the call inside OMO's un-capped internal loop.

## 3. Positions on disputed questions

**D1. Loop ownership.** The outer driver must own continuation and disable OMO's internal loops in `.omo/omo.jsonc` (`"goal": {"enabled": false}` and `disabled_hooks` blocking `goal`, `todo-continuation-enforcer`, and `boulder`). Use `bunx oh-my-openagent run --agent sisyphus --json --directory <worktree> <prompt>`, because `oh-my-openagent run --agent` resolves config keys (`sisyphus`) whereas `opencode run --agent` does exact-key host lookup against `Sisyphus - ultraworker` and fails closed ([omo CLI reference](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/dev/docs/reference/cli.md)).

**D2. omo target.** Stay pinned to `v4.19.4` OpenCode edition for this PR, because 5.x/`dev` splits into the `senpi`-based `omo-ai` binary and renames agents (`main agent`, `plan-consultant`, `plan-reviewer`, `deep-low`, `deep-high`) incompatibly with `scripts/materialize.py` ([omo dev features](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/dev/docs/reference/features.md)). Neither `v4.19.4` nor 5.x/`dev` enforces `goal.default_max_iterations` in code ([v4.19.4 `goal/index.ts`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/v4.19.4/packages/omo-opencode/src/hooks/goal/index.ts), [dev `goal/index.ts`](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/bd1310b65df8d772bba604e958e79956dc3e89a2/packages/omo-opencode/src/hooks/goal/index.ts)): `v4.19.4` falsely claimed a 100-iteration cap in docs, while `dev` updated its docs to admit the hook never enforces it.

**D3. Decision seat family.** A log-only shadow seat has no control authority and is exempt, but once a decider can dispatch, gate, or stop a run, it inherits Rule O2 (zero family overlap with any author, reviewer, or aggregator across all rungs and cumulative lineage). Open-weight deciders fine-tuned from Qwen or Gemma inherit their base family (`alibaba` or `google`), and closed deciders (`jev-1.14.0`, `classify-1`) must use explicit vendor prefixes (`typesafe/`, `scaledown/`) and fail validation if `unknown`.

**D4. Profile choice.** Profile selection (`b4b`, `hybrid`, `ultimate`, `global`) is an immutable owner policy fixed at admission, never a model decision or mid-run escalation ladder, because profiles enforce Hong Kong reachability (Rule O6) and data retention. Mid-run switching is forbidden because, as Situation A01 shows ([Matrix](https://github.com/bughunt8/model-mesh/blob/daf9ac42a9e86700a2f6c1c8b10353cd998a2a09/docs/research/orchestration/MATRIX.md)), it can assign a `deepseek` reviewer in `hybrid` to audit `deepseek`-authored code from an earlier `ultimate` iteration.

**D5. Done authority.** A decision model must never vote to approve completion; at most it may act as a negative-only smoke detector where `P(goal_met) < 0.50` when deterministic checks pass triggers an early cross-family review audit. Completion requires deterministic checks exiting zero, zero unresolved cross-family reviewer blockers (`mm-verify`), and the owner's merge gate.

**D6. Thresholds.** Point thresholds (`0.80` or `0.95`) may serve only as non-acting shadow log markers; activating any gate requires Learn-then-Test calibration on held-out local English and Chinese/Cantonese runs ([Confidence Is Not a Promise](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36311v1)). Because Jev Choice `confidence` (\((p_{\max} - 1/n)/(1 - 1/n)\)), ScaleDown Choice `confidence` (\(p_{\max}\)), and `noul` (\(P(\text{yes})\)) are distinct quantities ([TypeSafe confidence docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence.md), [ScaleDown Decisions API](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/decisions)), the driver must ingest raw `probabilities` and calibrate per `(backend, model_version, question_id)`.

**D7. Default caps for the pilot.** Hard defaults: **10 outer iterations**, **30 minutes wall clock** (5-minute per-iteration timeout), **2 consecutive no-progress iterations** (unchanged diff and check hash), **3 failed fix-verify cycles** on one finding fingerprint, and a **$5.00 USD** reserved spend cap. Any cap breach exits non-zero (`budget_exhausted` or `stalled`), never completion.

**D8. Validator.** Add decision-seat and superloop validator rules (`O19` to `O22`) in `scripts/validate-orchestration.py` (with `--self-test` mutations) in **this PR**. Merging a driver without validator checks for finite `max_iterations`, pinned model IDs, cumulative lineage independence, and O6 Hong Kong reachability invites policy bypass.

**D9. CDMs in this PR.** Include a shadow-only `scaledown` backend in `scripts/decision-client.py` (serializing state into `state.text` because `/v1/scaledown` reads only `state.text`/`state.document` and bills the sum of internal calls per [ScaleDown Decisions overview](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/decisions-overview)), and include **neither** `Extract` **nor** `Summarize`/`Compress` in the decision path. `Extract` has a character-versus-byte offset bug and a 30-entity cap ([ScaleDown Extract docs](https://docs.scaledown.ai/api-reference/extract)) that can drop error 31, while `Summarize` and `Compress` inject unverifiable generative rewrites into evidence.

**D10. "Endless".** The only defensible reading is: *"The controller loops unattended across fresh worker sessions without manual re-prompting until all deterministic checks and cross-family review gates pass, or a pre-declared iteration, time, stall, or budget cap halts the run as incomplete."*

**D11. Overall verdict on the hypothesis.** The hypothesis is **disproved in all six patterns for bounded control-flow forks** (each pattern has at least one gate, routing, triage, dispatch, or stopping decision where a typed classifier fits), while **holding strictly for the central orchestrator seat itself**, which must decompose tasks, write plans, and synthesize results in prose ([TypeSafe System One docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/concepts/system-one)).

## 4. Factual corrections to my round 1 report

1. **TypeSafe data retention.** In Round 1, I echoed `SYSTEM-ONE.md` in saying TypeSafe has "no retention policy." Re-verifying [TypeSafe Legal](https://docs.typesafe.ai/legal), the [TypeSafe DPA](https://typesafe.ai/legal/data-processing), and [TypeSafe Help Center](https://help.typesafe.ai/articles/5974925716-how-is-submitted-data-used-or-retained) shows TypeSafe publishes a DPA and Privacy Policy, forbids training on customer requests, and offers enterprise Zero Data Retention (ZDR); however, for non-ZDR accounts, DPA Schedule I Section 8 sets no numeric deletion window ("for as long as necessary... including laws on the statute of limitations") and allows telemetry and billing processing, so hosted Jev remains barred from `ultimate` without enterprise ZDR and verified Hong Kong reachability.
2. **Worker CLI invocation.** In Round 1, I wrote `opencode run --dir "<worktree>" --format json --agent sisyphus "<prompt>"`. Re-verifying the [OMO CLI reference](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/blob/dev/docs/reference/cli.md) shows `opencode run --agent` performs exact-key host lookup and misses `Sisyphus - ultraworker`, so the driver must call `bunx oh-my-openagent run --agent sisyphus --json --directory <worktree> <prompt>` with OMO continuation hooks disabled in `.omo/omo.jsonc`.
3. **Jev 1.14 and Laya token budgets.** `jev-1.14.0` enforces 64k-token per-request and 32k per-question default limits (262k via `TypeSafe-Danger-Zone-Long-Context: true` at lower speed and accuracy) ([TypeSafe models docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models)), and Laya's 512-token English context subtracts `head_max_len = 192` to leave **320 tokens for `state`** (768 of 1,024 on `laya-multilingual` and `laya-typed-decisions`) ([Laya model card](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya)).
4. **`madewithjev.com` attribution.** Our PR update to `docs/research/orchestration/SYSTEM-ONE.md` must note that [madewithjev.com](https://madewithjev.com/jev-multi-agent) is an independent site by Jon Kraayenbrink and unaffiliated with TypeSafe AI.
